# OpsNest
Finance and operations dashboard for engineering sites: clerks log expenses, invoices and payroll, management tracks cash flow, VAT and approvals in one place.

# Overview
Engineering companies run many sites at once, and the numbers are scattered across paper slips, spreadsheets and chat messages. OpsNest puts everything in one place with two clear roles:

Clerks record day-to-day expenses, invoices and monthly payroll. Gross and tax are calculated as they type, so there is no manual VAT arithmetic.
Management gets a live view of cash flow, VAT and pending approvals, and decides what stays on the books.

When you open the app, you pick a role to get started.

# Features
- Role-based access: separate views for Daily Ops clerks, Revenue & Payroll clerks and Management
- Expense entry in batches: add multiple lines (for example, a diesel purchase plus a second item) and submit them together
- Automatic gross and tax calculation while entering data
- Cash cockpit: a live dashboard showing today's spending, cash flow and VAT
- Payroll tracking per site: a red banner flags any site that is missing payroll for the month
- Payroll spread over working days: one monthly payroll entry is distributed across working days so daily figures stay realistic
- Cancellation workflow: clerks request cancellation of an entry with a reason, and management approves or rejects it
- Full audit trail: cancelled entries drop out of every total but stay on record

# Quick start: guided walkthrough
Follow these five steps to see the whole workflow end to end:

1. Log an expense as a clerk - Sign in as the Daily Ops clerk. Add a diesel purchase, add a second line, then submit the batch. Gross and tax are calculated as you type.

2. Watch the dashboard update - Switch to Management. Today's bar in the cash cockpit and the Daily Ops count already include the new entries.

3. Clear the red payroll banner- The banner says payroll is missing for two sites. Sign in as the Revenue & Payroll clerk and submit this month's payroll for one of them.

4. Check the spread - Back as Management, the banner now names one site. Click Payroll & Overheads to see the month as one entry spread over working days.

5. Ask to cancel an entry - As the Daily Ops clerk, open My entries and request cancellation of any line, with a reason.

6. Approve it - As Management, open Cancellation Requests and approve. The entry drops out of every total but stays on record.
